1. Definitions

"Agency" refers to Your Business Name. "Client" refers to the party named above. "Project" refers to the scope of work outlined in the agreed proposal. "Deliverables" means all files, designs, and assets produced. "Working days" means Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays.

2. Scope of Services & Deliverables

The Agency agrees to provide the following services as outlined in the agreed project proposal:

  • Website design & development (responsive — mobile, tablet, desktop)
  • UI/UX design — wireframes, visual design, and layout
  • Content integration (content provided by the Client)
  • Basic on-page SEO setup — meta tags, sitemap, heading structure
  • Cross-browser and cross-device testing
  • 3 rounds of revisions as specified in the proposal
  • Post-launch support period of 30 days
Scope Protection: Any request beyond the agreed scope — including new pages, additional design concepts, copywriting, advanced integrations, or significant structural changes — will be quoted separately and added only upon written approval.

3. Payment Schedule & Terms

A non-refundable deposit of 50% of the total project fee is required before work commences. The remaining balance is due upon project completion, before final files or site access are transferred.

  • Deposit due before project start
  • Milestone payment upon design sign-off (for larger projects)
  • Final balance before launch or file delivery
  • Payment due within 7 days of invoice issue
  • Late payments: 5% fee applied per 7-day period overdue
  • Continued non-payment may result in work suspension

4. Client Responsibilities

The Client agrees to provide the following in a timely manner to avoid project delays:

  • All content: text, images, videos, and brand assets
  • Prompt feedback and approvals at each milestone stage
  • Domain and hosting access where applicable
  • Accurate, legally compliant, and original content
  • A designated contact person for the duration of the project

5. Timeline & Client Delays

The estimated project timeline is outlined in the project proposal. The Agency will make every effort to meet agreed deadlines.

Client Delay Clause: If required materials, content, approvals, or feedback are delayed by the Client beyond 3 working days, the project timeline may shift accordingly. The Agency is not responsible for missed deadlines caused by delayed client communication or content delivery.

6. Revisions, Changes & Final Approval

This agreement includes 3 rounds of revisions. Each round must be submitted as a single, consolidated list. Additional revisions beyond the agreed number will be billed at $50/hr.

Final Approval Clause: Once the Client provides written final approval, any further edits or changes will be treated as a new task or maintenance request, unless covered within the post-launch support period.

7. Intellectual Property & Ownership

Upon receipt of full and final payment, the Client receives full ownership of all final deliverables produced for this project. The Agency retains ownership of all work until payment is received in full.

  • The Agency retains the right to display completed work in its portfolio and marketing materials
  • Third-party assets (fonts, stock images, plugins) remain subject to their respective licences
  • The Client is responsible for ensuring they hold rights to all content they provide

8. Cancellation & Termination

Either party may terminate this agreement with 7 days written notice.

  • The initial deposit is non-refundable upon cancellation
  • All completed work to the cancellation date will be invoiced and due immediately
  • Partial work will not be transferred until all outstanding payments are settled

9. Refund Policy

The initial deposit is non-refundable as it secures the Agency's time and project slot. Should the Agency fail to deliver the agreed services without reasonable cause, a partial refund may be negotiated in good faith for work not yet completed.

10. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep all project details, business information, and client data confidential. Neither party will disclose proprietary information to third parties without prior written consent, except where required by law.

11. Limitation of Liability

The Agency's total liability shall not exceed the total fees paid for the project. The Agency is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages — including loss of revenue, data, or business — arising from use of the delivered work.

12. Hosting, Domain & Third-Party Costs

Unless explicitly included in the agreed package, the Client is responsible for hosting, domain, and any third-party software licence costs. The Agency accepts no liability for third-party service outages, updates, or policy changes.

13. Browser & Device Compatibility

The website will be tested on the latest versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge across standard screen sizes. The Agency does not guarantee compatibility with outdated browsers or custom enterprise environments.

14. Legal Compliance & Content Accuracy

The Client is solely responsible for ensuring all content provided is accurate, truthful, and legally compliant — including privacy policies, GDPR compliance, copyright compliance, and advertising standards. The Agency accepts no liability for content provided by the Client.

15. Force Majeure & Governing Law

Neither party shall be held liable for delays caused by events beyond reasonable control. This agreement is governed by the laws of Your Country / Region. Disputes shall first be addressed through good-faith negotiation before any formal proceedings.
